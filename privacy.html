Privacy policy

Effective October 27, 2026 · Remotoking by Be Something

Remotoking is an iPhone remote for supported TVs and Fire TV. No Remotoking account is required. Its core device discovery, pairing and remote commands run over your local network.

Information kept on your device

The app stores the rooms and names you enter, device addresses and models, TV input links, preferences and saved pairing information needed to reconnect. Pairing credentials use the iPhone’s secure storage. Discovery and control exchange information with the TV or Fire TV you choose on your local network.

Remotoking does not operate a cloud service that stores your room layouts or routes your TV commands. You can remove saved devices or use the app’s reset settings to remove the Remotoking records stored by the app on that iPhone. Disabling Local Network access in iPhone Settings prevents local device discovery and control.

Apple purchases

When purchase plans are available, Apple processes payment using your Apple Account. Remotoking uses Apple’s StoreKit to verify current purchase access on the device and restore eligible purchases. We do not receive your card number or Apple Account password, and the app does not send purchase receipts or transaction identifiers to a Remotoking billing server. Apple handles its own transaction information under Apple’s privacy policy.

Optional support

If you choose to contact support, we receive the information in the email you send, including your email address and any issue description, model information or screenshots you choose to include. Your email provider and our support mailbox provider process that correspondence.

The app does not automatically send a support report. An optional connection report excludes network addresses, pairing codes and pairing secrets. Review your message and attachments before sending; please do not include passwords, pairing PINs or sensitive personal information.

We use support correspondence to answer questions and investigate compatibility issues. We retain it only as reasonably needed for that support and issue history, or when required by applicable obligations. To ask about or request deletion of support correspondence, email be.something.llc@gmail.com.

Backups you choose to share

Room preset exports are initiated by you and exclude pairing credentials. An export can contain your room and device names and input layout, so share it only with recipients you choose. Importing presets on another phone does not transfer device pairing credentials.

Tracking and this website

Remotoking does not use advertising trackers or sell your personal information. This support website has no added advertising or analytics scripts. It is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub may process technical request information under the GitHub privacy statement.

Questions and changes

For privacy questions, contact be.something.llc@gmail.com. We may update this policy as the app changes and will update the effective date on this page.
